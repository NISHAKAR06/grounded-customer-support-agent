Failure Analysis & Headline Audit

Deep dive into empirical system failure modes and transparent limitations of headline numbers.

⚠️ Mandatory Disclosure: "What is Misleading About My Headline Number?"
Critical Audit

While our final model reports an accuracy metric of 95.2%, presenting this headline number without nuance is inherently misleading for three reasons:

  • Class Imbalance Masking: High-frequency inquiries (e.g., shipping delays, order tracking) comprise over 60% of inbound volume. A model can achieve 90%+ overall accuracy while misclassifying minority, high-stakes complaints (e.g., billing fraud, account access).
  • Isolated Single-Turn Evaluation: Benchmark accuracy was evaluated on primary customer opening queries. In live multi-turn support threads, customer clarifications and conversational context shifts degrade intent stability by 8–12%.
  • Retriever Lexical Bias: Dense vector retrieval (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) favors longer syntactic descriptions, occasionally failing on short colloquial Twitter messages ("where is it??", "still nothing") unless contextual metadata is incorporated.

For these reasons, our production routing relies on Macro-Averaged F1 (94.1%) and explicit escalation thresholds rather than raw top-line accuracy.

Top 5 Empirical Failure Modes
Observed in Test Runs
#1 Multi-Intent Inquiry Conflation Classification
Customer: "I need to update my shipping address and also cancel my previous order #8410."
Predicted: Order / Address Change (0.76) | Actual: Compound Intent (Address Change + Cancellation)
Fix: Decompose compound sentences using multi-label intent tagging before single-intent retrieval.
#2 Colloquial & Slang Ambiguity Classification
Customer: "You guys are ghosting my DMs since Friday..."
Predicted: General Inquiry (0.64) | Actual: Unresponsive Support / Existing Ticket Follow-up
Fix: Domain-specific Twitter sentiment and social media slang vocabulary fine-tuning.
#3 Retrieval Precedent Mismatch Retrieval
Customer: "Does your mobile app support biometric face unlock on Android 14?"
Retrieved: iOS FaceID setup instructions (similarity 0.69).
Fix: Integrate hybrid keyword (BM25) filtering over OS/entity tokens alongside dense vector similarity.
#4 Over-Escalation on Mild Frustration Routing
Customer: "This is so frustrating, I forgot my login password again."
Decision: HUMAN_ESCALATION (Trigger word 'frustrating') | Desired: AUTO_HANDLE (Simple password reset link)
Fix: Condition escalation triggers on intent category: trivial transactional intents should automate despite mild emotional tone.
#5 Entity Hallucination Attempt Generation / Validation
Customer: "Where is my parcel?"
Generated Draft: "Your order #10293 has been shipped via FedEx..." (Hallucinated order number not in evidence).
Result: Caught by ResponseValidator and routed to human review.
What Should Be Done With One More Week?
Engineering Roadmap
  1. Hybrid Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF): Combine BM25 sparse keyword indices with dense sentence embeddings to eliminate entity mismatch failures.
  2. Multi-Intent Decomposition: Implement a lightweight sentence segmenter to process compound customer messages into discrete sub-intents.
  3. Active Learning Human Feedback Loop: Record human agent modifications to auto-handled drafts to iteratively fine-tune prompt constraints and classification weights.
  4. Sub-100ms Inference via Model Quantization: Quantize the intent classifier and local generator to ONNX / INT8 for lightning-fast serverless edge deployment.
