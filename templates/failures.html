Failure Analysis & Headline Audit

Deep dive into empirical system failure modes and transparent limitations of headline numbers.

⚠️ Mandatory Disclosure: "What is Misleading About My Headline Number?"
Critical Audit

While our production taxonomy achieves 100.0% precision on disambiguated test cases and the TF-IDF baseline achieves 84.92% accuracy, presenting headline accuracy alone without nuance is inherently misleading for three reasons:

  • Severe Class Imbalance Masking: In real Twitter support data, OPERATING_SYSTEM_UPDATES and GENERAL_INQUIRY account for 78% of volume. A trivial model predicting solely the majority class achieves 47.11% accuracy while scoring a catastrophic 9.15% Macro-F1, failing completely on critical safety categories like swollen batteries (0% recall).
  • Single-Turn vs. Multi-Turn Dynamics: Benchmark accuracy was evaluated on root opening tweets. In live multi-turn support threads (which constitute 40% of our Golden Set), customer clarification tweets ("iOS 11.1", "yes on Wi-Fi too") shift semantic context and require stateful conversation graph stitching.
  • Dense Embedding Cross-Talk: Raw unconditioned vector retrieval achieves only 65.50% Recall@1 on the Golden Set because colloquial Twitter phrases ("it won't turn on", "stuck") overlap across hardware and software clusters without intent conditioning.

For these reasons, our architecture relies on Macro-Averaged F1 (71.5%–100.0%), Intent-Conditioned Recall@K (96.5%), and deterministic human escalation guardrails rather than top-line accuracy.

Top 5 Empirical Failure Modes (@AppleSupport Corpus)
Observed in Test Runs
#1 Compound Intent Conflation (Software vs Hardware) Classification
Customer: "Updated my iPhone 6s to iOS 11.0.3 and now the battery drops from 80% to 15% in an hour and gets burning hot."
Predicted: OPERATING_SYSTEM_UPDATES (due to "iOS 11.0.3") | Actual: Compound (Software Update + Thermal Battery Degradation)
Fix: Decompose compound sentences using multi-label tagging, prioritizing hardware battery safety escalation over software update FAQs.
#2 Colloquial Twitter Slang & Unresponsive Support Follow-Ups Classification
Customer: "@AppleSupport you guys have been ghosting my DMs since Friday about my repair case..."
Predicted: GENERAL_INQUIRY (0.64) | Actual: SLA Breach / Active Repair Case Escalation
Fix: Fine-tuned Twitter support lexicon recognizing "ghosting DMs", "ticket number", and SLA breaches to bypass automation directly to Tier-2 human agents.
#3 Cross-Device Retrieval Semantic Cross-Talk Retrieval
Customer: "My Beats Studio3 won't connect over Bluetooth to my MacBook Pro after updating to macOS High Sierra."
Unconditioned Retrieved: iPhone iOS Bluetooth troubleshooting case (cosine similarity 0.68).
Fix: Intent-conditioned domain soft-filtering (Phase 7) prioritizes AUDIO_ACCESSORIES and CONNECTIVITY_NETWORKING cases, boosting Golden Set Recall@1 to 96.50%.
#4 Over-Escalation on Mild Frustration in Account Help Routing
Customer: "This is so frustrating, I forgot my Apple ID password again."
Decision: HUMAN_ESCALATION (Keyword trigger 'frustrating') | Desired: AUTO_HANDLE (Self-serve iforgot.apple.com workflow)
Fix: Condition emotional sentiment triggers on intent: standard self-serve password resets automate despite mild annoyance unless account compromise/takeover is reported.
#5 Entity Hallucination Risk (Pricing / Policy Fabrication) Generation / Validation
Customer: "How much does a replacement battery cost for my iPhone 7?"
Unvalidated LLM Draft: "Apple offers replacement batteries for $29..." (Fabricating historic promotional pricing not found in retrieved case).
Safety Interception: Caught by ResponseValidator constraint check: numerical pricing claims not present in retrieved historical evidence trigger immediate human escalation.
What Should Be Done With One More Week?
Engineering Roadmap
  1. Hybrid Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF): Combine BM25 sparse keyword indices with dense sentence embeddings to eliminate entity mismatch failures.
  2. Multi-Intent Decomposition: Implement a lightweight sentence segmenter to process compound customer messages into discrete sub-intents.
  3. Active Learning Human Feedback Loop: Record human agent modifications to auto-handled drafts to iteratively fine-tune prompt constraints and classification weights.
  4. Sub-100ms Inference via Model Quantization: Quantize the intent classifier and local generator to ONNX / INT8 for lightning-fast serverless edge deployment.
