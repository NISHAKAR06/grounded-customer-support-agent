Methodology & System Specifications

Formal mathematical formulations, interactive intent taxonomy, and deterministic escalation policies.

1. Grounding-First Generation Principle
Strict Architectural Rule

A response $\hat{y}$ generated for an incoming customer message $q$ is defined as Grounded if and only if:

Claims(ŷ) ⊆ Claims(q) ∪ Claims(E)

Where $E = \{e_1, e_2, \dots, e_k\}$ is the set of top-$k$ retrieved historical resolved support interactions from the brand, and $\text{Claims}(\cdot)$ extracts factual commitments (e.g., refund policies, turnaround times, troubleshooting procedures).

Any claim $c \in \text{Claims}(\hat{y})$ that cannot be mapped to $E$ or $q$ constitutes an ungrounded hallucination and is blocked by the ResponseValidator.

2. Escalation Policy Decision Matrix
Deterministic Formulation

The routing decision $D \in \{\text{AUTO\_HANDLE}, \text{HUMAN\_ESCALATION}\}$ is computed via the boolean evaluation:

D = AUTO_HANDLE   if   [P(y = c_intent | q) ≥ τ_intent] ∧ [max_{e ∈ E} cos(e, q) ≥ τ_retrieval] ∧ [¬RiskTrigger(q)] ∧ [ValidatorPassed(ŷ)]
D = HUMAN_ESCALATION   otherwise
  • $\tau_{\text{intent}}$: Minimum calibrated intent classifier confidence (default: 0.85).
  • $\tau_{\text{retrieval}}$: Minimum cosine similarity of top historical case (default: 0.65).
  • $\text{RiskTrigger}(q)$: Boolean function detecting explicit human agent requests, legal threats, or severe dispute language.
  • $\text{ValidatorPassed}(\hat{y})$: Output of deterministic non-empty, claim-bounded validation check.

4. Disambiguation & Tie-Breaking Priority Hierarchy
Precedence Matrix

When customer utterances span multiple semantic categories simultaneously, deterministic tie-breaking rules guarantee consumer safety, policy compliance, and financial liability prevention. Click any card to test the conflict live:

1. Safety Hazard & Physical Damage Priority Rank 1 Precedence
Rule: Physical danger (swollen battery, burning hot chassis, shattered display) strictly overrides software update or general queries.
Conflict Query: "After installing the iOS 11 update my iPhone battery became swollen and pushed the screen out." → BATTERY_POWER_HARDWARE
2. Financial Dispute & Liability Priority Rank 2 Precedence
Rule: Unauthorized iTunes charges, unwanted subscription renewals, and refund requests strictly override store or general inquiries.
Conflict Query: "I checked my receipt and noticed an unauthorized subscription charge from iTunes billing, need refund." → SUBSCRIPTIONS_BILLING
3. Identity & Security Lockout Priority Rank 3 Precedence
Rule: 2FA failures, Apple ID disables, and password recovery lockouts take precedence over OS update downloads.
Conflict Query: "I cannot update any apps because my Apple ID has been disabled for security reasons." → ACCOUNT_APPLE_ID
4. Peripheral Specificity Over Generic Protocol Rank 6 vs Rank 5
Rule: Audio hardware issues (AirPods, headphones, microphone) over Bluetooth take precedence over generic networking.
Conflict Query: "My AirPods won't connect via Bluetooth to my MacBook." → AUDIO_ACCESSORIES
5. Ambiguity Fallback Rank 7 Fallback
Rule: Inquiries without domain-specific technical signals or confidence < 0.50 default to General Support for human routing.
Conflict Query: "Can someone please help me right now?" → GENERAL_INQUIRY
